## 🌍 Project Pitch (Attendance Control)

Many universities still rely on paper sheets or disconnected spreadsheets to record student attendance, which leads to errors, lost data, and delays when calculating absence rates. This creates frustration for both teachers, who spend extra time on manual tracking, and students, who often cannot verify their own attendance record in time to react. AsistApp is a hybrid mobile application, built with Ionic and React, designed to solve this problem with a simple, role-based experience for two clearly defined user types. The target users are university teachers, who need a fast and reliable way to record attendance for each class session, and students, who need quick access to their attendance history and percentage without depending on manual reports from instructors. The MVP focuses on three core features: role-based login, attendance registration by teachers (marking each student as present, absent, or late), and an attendance history view for students that includes their overall attendance percentage per course. Advanced features such as automatic notifications, detailed statistics dashboards, QR-based check-in, or facial recognition are intentionally left out of this first version, so the team can validate the core workflow with real users before investing time in additional complexity.
