# Movie-rater

**Proyect pitch**

Movie-rater is a simple mobile application designed to help users add movies and rate their favorite films. Many people struggle to keep track of what they watch and the ratings they gave in the past. Our target users are movie lovers who want a fast and organized way to build a personal list of titles. The MVP focuses on three core actions: adding a movie, assigning a rating, and viewing the list of saved movies with their ratings. With movie-rater, users can quickly reflect their preferences and revisit their favorite films anytime.
