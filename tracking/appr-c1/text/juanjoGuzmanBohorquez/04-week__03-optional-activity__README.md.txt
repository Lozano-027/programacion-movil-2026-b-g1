# 🚀 StudyTasks — Academic Task Management

> A lightweight, purpose-built space designed for students to track deadlines, lower cognitive friction, and elevate academic performance.

---

## 📊 Overview

| Attribute | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Target Audience** | High school & university students (Ages 13–25) |
| **Project Stage** | MVP (Minimum Viable Product) |
| **Primary Goal** | Centralized academic task tracking & deadline management |
| **Excluded (MVP)** | Complex calendar sync, analytics dashboards, 3rd-party integrations |

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Academic success requires managing high volumes of simultaneous responsibilities—assignments, readings, exams, and projects. Most students rely on fragmented methods like physical notes, scattered chat threads, or simple memory.

This lack of centralized organization directly leads to:
* ⚠️ **Missed deadlines** and poor time allocation.
* 📈 **Elevated cognitive load** and unnecessary stress.
* 📉 **Avoidable drops in overall academic performance.**

---

## 💡 The Solution

**StudyTasks** provides a centralized, distraction-free environment for students to manage their academic workload effortlessly. Unlike generic to-do apps or heavy project management tools, StudyTasks focuses strictly on core academic execution.

### 🎯 Core MVP Capabilities
1. **Task Lifecycle Management:** Quickly create and edit tasks with essential details (title, subject tag, and explicit target dates).
2. **Smart Deadline Reminders:** Timely alerts delivered ahead of due dates to keep students on schedule.
3. **Progress Tracking:** Interactive workflow allowing users to mark tasks as completed and audit remaining responsibilities.
