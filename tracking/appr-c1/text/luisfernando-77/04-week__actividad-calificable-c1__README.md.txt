# 📱 FilaFácil 

## Project Pitch

FilaFácil is a mobile application designed to solve the problem of long and disorganized waiting lines in small establishments such as coffee shops, offices, and local events. The main target users are busy customers who want to save time and business owners looking to improve their service flow. The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) focuses on three core capabilities: requesting a digital turn token directly from a smartphone, tracking the queue status in real time to see how many people are ahead, and receiving push notifications when the turn is approaching. By streamlining the waiting experience, FilaFácil removes physical crowding and enhances user satisfaction.
